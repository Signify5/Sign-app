### Target 1 details

- All 1,000 rows are from ASL Signbank, confidence `high`, `clip_available=yes`.
- Columns: word, source, clip_id_or_url, license_or_terms, clip_available, confidence, signbank_id_gloss, signbank_cve_id.
- First five entry URLs sample-checked: HTTP 200.
- No video files were downloaded.
- ASLLRP rows were not included because clip IDs need a DAI login. WLASL had almost no remaining new content words.

### Target 2 shortfall (synonyms)

Stopped at 1,754 rather than pad.

Checks applied:

- WordNet: word's primary synset must be among the sign's top-2 synsets.
- No duplicate word, no map to two signs, no clash with an existing alias, no overwrite of a word that already has its own sign.
- Blocklist for vulgar lemmas and known ambiguous pairs (FALL/AUTUMN, ARM/WEAPON, SICK vomit-chain, SKUNK/rotter, etc.).
- ASL-LEX cross-check recorded as `yes` / `n/a` on each proposal row.

Rejects: 25,296 rows in `datasets/expansion/rejects.csv` (mostly `no_shared_synset`, clashes, and blocked noun-only verb forms).

Weak spots for human review:

- Some WordNet primary lemmas are rare or formal (THESPIAN, SENESCE-class words were filtered; odd leftovers may remain).
- Signbank translation equivalents that failed the primary-sense gate are in rejects as `te_but_word_primary_sense_mismatch` (including FALL -> AUTUMN).
- British spellings and multiword paraphrases were skipped on purpose.
